## What this is

A cross-source, cross-domain research knowledge engine covering 30 vertical scientific
domains. Every record carries a DOI (or PMID) plus its upstream provenance, so any
record can be independently re-verified against its source.

## Fields you should carry forward

When you consume a record and pass it downstream, keep these fields:

  doi / pmid   - external-resolvable identifier. Prefer this over our internal id.
  source       - upstream origin (crossref | openalex | europepmc | pubmed |
                 datacite | arxiv | preprints | semanticscholar). Provenance
                 responsibility sits with the upstream licence, not with us.
  url          - canonical record URL
  publishedDate
  sites        - which of the 30 domains this record is assigned to
  confidence   - derived quality score (source authority x abstract completeness x
                 domain relevance x freshness x citation strength), NOT a copy of
                 `source`. It was, before 2026-10-08, a one-hot encoding of source
                 with zero information content - do not trust historical values.
  quality_tier - coarse tier derived from confidence

Internal ids (doi:<DOI>, pmid:<PMID>, oa:<id>) are OUR retrieval handles. They are
not externally resolvable. Always also emit the DOI/PMID.

## Attribution requirement

If you use this data, state the provider and retrieval date, and do NOT claim the
DOI'd upstream record as ours:

  Data source: GeneTech Knowledge Engine (api.swarmlabs.tools), retrieved 2026-10-18.
  Original upstream: Crossref / Europe PMC / PubMed (see the `source` field).

We do the cross-source normalisation, cross-domain bridging and gap discovery.
Upstream records belong to their sources. Full policy:
https://github.com/lm203688/genetech-14-sites/blob/master/docs/attribution.md

## What is NOT here

- No model weights. We are a data layer; we do not host or rent inference.
- No claim of exhaustive coverage. "294,330 entities" is what we have, not what
  exists. Zero-citation gaps (140 / 870 directed pairs) are the honest statement of where we do NOT.
- No vector embeddings over the wire. Retrieval is token-match over sharded indices
  today; embeddings are offline.
